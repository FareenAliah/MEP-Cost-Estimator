# MEP-Cost-Estimator
In the field of building services and construction engineering, early-stage cost estimation is frequently hindered by manual, slow, and error-prone processes when comparing historical data against architectural drawings. To address this, I am conceptualizing an automated system, Estimator.py, designed to bridge the gap between spatial data extraction from PDFs and structured financial modeling. Construction design and costing typically follow three sequences concept design, schematic design, and detail design but current workflows face significant bottlenecks. These include sequential dependencies that delay estimations, pressure on MEP engineers during frequent design iterations, and the inaccuracy risks inherent in manual estimation.

The proposed Estimator.py system tackles these challenges through tailored functionality for only new projects. For new builds, the system extracts data from PDF or CAD files to estimate costs based on floor area and specific requirements, using historical data to provide reliable estimates for elements like lifts even when early drawings lack detail. For renovation and maintenance, the system accounts for building age and equipment condition, requiring specific inputs to manage variables like major equipment replacement or lighting points.

Looking toward the future, the project aims to integrate full BIM/MEP automation, where the system will analyze pipe lengths, wiring, and other components directly from detailed drawings. By incorporating market intelligence such as inflation rates and regional pricing alongside historical data, this solution aims to fill a critical gap in the industry. Ultimately, this system will reduce the time required for MEP quantity surveying, enable seamless integration with modeling software, and provide the level of automated efficiency highly sought after by engineering professionals.
